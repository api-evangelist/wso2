Philippine banks need to establish trust infrastructure before new BSP open finance regulatory standards arrive, since architectural decisions made now determine which institutions can absorb regulatory change without major system rebuilds. Banks that prioritize composable identity infrastructure handling both human and machine access under unified policies will adapt more smoothly than those retrofitting identity systems beneath existing APIs.
